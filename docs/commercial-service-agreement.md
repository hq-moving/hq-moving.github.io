## 3. PRICING & BILLING

### 3.1 Pricing Method

All work under this Agreement is billed on a **Time & Materials (T&M)** basis at the all-inclusive hourly rates in **Exhibit B**. Final charges are based on actual billable hours worked, not estimates.

### 3.2 All-Inclusive Hourly Rates

| Crew Size | Rate (Per Person) | Total Crew Rate |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| 2-person crew | $70.00 / hour | $140.00 / hour |
| 3-person crew | $65.00 / hour | $195.00 / hour |

Rates include labor, standard hand and power tools, dollies, hand trucks, standard mounting fasteners/anchors, workers compensation, general liability insurance, and local travel within the Treasure Coast service area when no commercial moving truck is required.

**Minimum charge:** Four (4) billable hours per crew mobilization unless otherwise stated in Exhibit A.

**After-hours / weekend premium:** Fifteen percent (15%) added to labor rates when work is performed outside Customer’s standard access window if not included in the base quote.

### 3.3 Billing Clock (Commercial On-Site Services)

Customer acknowledges and agrees that for commercial furniture and facility services:

**Billing time begins** when Company’s crew arrives at the Service Address (or Customer-designated check-in point) and **continues uninterrupted until** all agreed work for that mobilization is complete and the crew demobilizes from the Service Address.

Billable time includes, without limitation:

- Check-in, security, and escort waits  
- Site orientation and layout walkthroughs directed by Customer  
- Moving, arranging, staging, and organizing furniture and equipment  
- On-campus relocation to storage areas designated by Customer  
- Furniture assembly and disassembly  
- Whiteboard and smart board mounting (mount-only scope unless Exhibit A states otherwise)  
- Light cleanup of work areas  
- Elevator waits, long carries, stair carries, and parking/loading distance  
- Customer-directed holds, re-layouts, or access delays  
- Weather delays while crew is on site and ready to work  
- Additional rooms, boards, or items not disclosed in Exhibit A  

**Billing time does not include** crew meal breaks of thirty (30) minutes or less when mutually taken, unless crew remains on site and unavailable due to Customer delay.

For projects requiring a commercial truck or off-site transport, travel and drive time related to that transport will be listed separately in Exhibit A or a Change Order.

### 3.4 Estimates Are Not Guaranteed Prices

Any verbal, written, email, or phone estimate is a **non-binding projection** based on information provided by Customer. **Actual billable time controls final charges**, regardless of original estimate, expected duration, or Customer expectations.

### 3.5 Change Orders

Work outside Exhibit A requires a written Change Order (email confirmation from Customer’s authorized contact is acceptable) before Company is obligated to perform additional work. Change Orders may adjust crew size, schedule, and estimated hours.

## 5. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITIES

Customer agrees to:

1. **Designate an on-site authorized representative** available during service to provide direction, approve layout/staging, and sign daily sign-off sheets.  
2. **Provide safe, unobstructed access** to all work areas, including keys, badges, elevators, loading areas, and storage locations.  
3. **Disclose** wall types, hidden utilities, fragile items, weight limits, and building rules before mounting or moving begins.  
4. **Furnish** all customer-supplied items identified in Exhibit A (boards, mounts, brackets, furniture, labels, floor plans, cleaning supplies if not provided by Company).  
5. **Confirm** whether storage is on-campus. On-campus storage moves do not require a commercial moving truck unless stated in Exhibit A.  
6. **Notify Company in advance** of occupied rooms, active classes, alarm codes, security procedures, and required COI endorsements.  
7. **Not perform physical moving work** alongside Company crew unless expressly agreed in writing (Customer staff may direct and inspect only).  
8. **Report damage or concerns before crew demobilizes** for that work session.

Customer is responsible for accuracy of scope information. Under-reporting rooms, boards, furniture quantities, or access constraints may increase time and cost without invalidating Company’s right to full payment for actual billable time.

## 7. DAMAGE, CLAIMS & LIMITATIONS

1. **Immediate notice required:** All damage, loss, or service concerns must be reported to Company’s lead on site **before the crew demobilizes** for that session. Claims not reported at that time may be denied.  
2. **Pre-existing condition:** Company is not responsible for pre-existing damage, concealed defects, unstable furniture, failed wall anchors due to undisclosed hollow/block conditions, or manufacturer defects in Customer-supplied equipment.  
3. **Mounting limitation:** Company mounts boards and displays using Customer-supplied or approved hardware. Company does not warrant wall suitability unless a pre-installation wall assessment was included in Exhibit A.  
4. **Liability cap (unless prohibited by law):** Customer agrees Company’s total liability for property damage directly caused by Company’s negligence shall not exceed the lesser of (a) the actual invoice for the service date on which the claim arose, or (b) $5,000.00, except where a higher limit is required by applicable law.  
5. **Consequential damages:** Neither party shall be liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including lost profits or business interruption, except as prohibited by law.
