## How this estimate works

- **Billing:** Flat **crew hourly rate** (total for the whole crew, not per person).
- **Clock:** Starts at crew arrival on campus; ends at demobilize (per commercial agreement).
- **Minimum:** 4 hours per mobilization.
- **Included:** Labor, tools, standard anchors, commercial insurance, local travel (no truck unless noted).
- **Excluded:** Customer-supplied boards/mounts, power/data/AV, off-site haul, cleaning supplies (pass-through if we provide).
